Frequently asked questions

A few details before your appointment.

How long does a full lash set take?

Most full sets take about 2 to 3 hours, depending on the style, natural lashes, and the look we are creating.

How often should I book a fill?

Fills are usually recommended every 2 to 3 weeks to keep your lashes full, balanced, and fresh.

How should I arrive for my appointment?

Please arrive with clean lashes and no eye makeup. This helps with retention and gives us the best foundation for your set.

Can I get my lashes wet after?

Yes, but follow the aftercare guidance given at your appointment. Keeping your lashes clean is one of the best ways to protect retention.

What if I am not sure what style to book?

Book the closest service and we can talk through your desired look before starting. I will help choose a style that fits your eyes and routine.

Why is a card required to book?

A valid card reserves your appointment time and supports the cancellation and no-show policy. Your card is saved securely through Stripe.

Can service be refused?

Yes. Lillyanna reserves the right to refuse service if studio policies are not followed, boundaries are not respected, or the appointment environment becomes unsafe.
